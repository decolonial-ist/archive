Dear Customer:

         Responding to our many customers’ requests, NTIS is offering FBIS publications electronically. Due
to resource limitations, hardcopy production of FBIS publications will be phased out during 1996. Hardcopy
reports which have already been discontinued include Arms Control & Proliferation Issues, Environment &
World Health, Narcotics, and Terrorism.

Hardcopy publication of the regional DAILY REPORTS will cease ar follows:

                  1 August       China,
                                   Latin America
                  19 August    Near East and South Asia, West Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa,
                                West Europe Economic  Review
                  2 September East Asia, East Europe, East Europe Economic Review
                  16 September Central Eurasia, Central Eurasia Military Affairs,
                                Central Eurasia Economic  Review

         FBIS’ goal is to cease publication of all hardcopy reports by 31 December 1996 except for S&T PER-
SPECTIVES, S&F CENTRAL EURASIA, S&T CHINA, S&T KOREA, S&T EUROPE, and S&T JAPAN.
The S&T reports will continue to be published as hardcopy until the graphics they contain can be disseminated
on-line.

"World News Connection” (WNC). This is a new on-line subscription service accessible through the World
Wide Web. The Web address is http://wnc.fedworld.gov. Piease see the next page for a subscription form or
call NTIS Fax Direct at 703-487-4140 and enter product code 8645 to receive more information.
