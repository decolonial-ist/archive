Estonia: Premier Discusses Current Issues With
U.S. Envoy

WS2407132496 Tallinn BNS in English
1000 GMT 24 Jul 96

{[FBIS Transcribed Text) Tallinn, Jul 24, BNS —
Estonia considers it possible to successfully finish
border talks with Russia before the end of this year,
Prime Minister Tiit Vahi said during a meeting with
U.S. Ambassador-at-Large James Collins.

If there is mutual good will, the talks can be finished and
a border agreement concluded after the delegations have
completed work on technical matters in September, Vahi
was quoted by the government press office as saying.

The prime minister said the fact that technical work
could be finished soon showed that Estonia had no
territorial claims to Russia. But Vahi also stressed the
importance of the Tartu peace treaty, which Russia has
refused to mention as still valid in the new border treaty.

Collins, in Estonia for a visit, asked about the draft
agreement on preference trade terms between Estonia
and Russia, and whether it was possible to implement
the accord in the near future.

Vahi expressed hope that the treaty on most-favored
Status in trade can be signed this year too.

The Estonian prime minister said he saw good opportu-
nities for improving the relations with Russia if Viktor
Chernomyrdin continues as prime minister. He said the
relations between the two countries should be based on
pragmatism, a good sense of reality and good will.

Also discussed during the meeting were U.S. invest-
ments in Estonia. Collins said major American corpora-
tions were interested in making investments in Russia.

Vahi spoke at some length about efforts of the Esto-
nian government aimed at the integration of Russian-
speakers into the country’s society.

The U.S. ambassador-at-large said Estonia's policy
toward ethnic Russians was fully in line with the
requirements set by the international community.

Collins, in the Estonian capital until Wednesday morn-
ing, also met with President Lennart Meri and Foreign
Minister Siim Kallas.

Estonia: Government Approves Sea Border
Agreement With Latvia

LD2407181196 Moscow INTERFAX in English
1529 GMT 24 Jul 96

(From the "Presidential Bulletin” feature]

(FBIS Transcribed Text] The Estonian Government
Tuesday approved an agreement on the sea border with
Latvia and decided to forward it to the parliament for
ratification, Estonian Foreign Minister Siim Kallas told
a news conference in Tallinn.

The agreement provides for the division of sea territories
and economic zones of the two countries in the Bay of
Riga and is intended to put an end to the problems
caused when Estonia unilaterally established a sea
border in 1993. As a result, Latvian fishermen were
deprived of a traditional fishing region near the Estonian
island of Ruhnu.

Latvian Prime Minister Andris Skele and Estonian
Prime Minister Tiit Vahi signed the agreement in early
July.
