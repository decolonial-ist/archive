Campaigns

  • Failed Attempt at Religious Subjugation of the Highlanders to the Empire through the Hiring of the Preacher Taj ad-Din Mustafin (1836-1841)
    The preacher's task was formulated openly: "to turn the highlanders onto the true path, subordinate them to the Russian government, and destroy or hand over Shamil to us alive" (Shamil - imam, leader of the Dagestani and Chechen highlanders' resistance to the Russian conquest)
    The project failed: Golovin, who succeeded Rozen, agreed to release the preacher based on a report by Minister of War Chernyshev of 13 November 1841 - "the teaching of Tajudin Effendi over the course of 6 years did not change the highlanders' attitude toward us in the slightest"
  • Forcing Chechnya into submission after the fall of Akhulgo and suppression of the general uprising of 1840 (1838-1840)
    …admitted by general Golovin himself - in a memorandum of 26 April 1841 on the results of the previous year's expedition: "In vain did we expect that after the destruction, in last year's expedition, of the highlanders' food stores, their villages and various property, hunger, cold and extreme poverty would shake the fierce power of the instigator" ("instigator" - the imperial label for Shamil)
  • "Constriction of Chechnya from all sides": blockade, secret war, punitive expeditions and the Sunzha Line (1840-1846)
    Pokrovsky formulates the policy's calculation from the documents: ""Starving Chechnya, by tsarism's reckoning, was to surrender in order to restore trade relations and obtain grain""

Procedures

  • Failed Attempt at Religious Subjugation of the Highlanders to the Empire through the Hiring of the Preacher Taj ad-Din Mustafin (1836-1841) — technique Creation of Puppet Religious Institutions
    Pokrovsky wrote: "a broad project emerges for subordinating the entire mountain Sunni clergy to a special spiritual person"; the task set was "to turn the highlanders onto the true path, subordinate them to the Russian government, and destroy or hand over Shamil to us alive" (Shamil - imam, leader of the Dagestani and Chechen highlanders' resistance to the Russian conquest)
    Only the establishment of the post, the appointment, and the preacher's years-long payment took place; subordinating the clergy did not succeed: "The preacher did not dare to show himself in the mountains"
    The empire wound down the project only six years later: Golovin, who succeeded Rozen, agreed to release Mustafin based on a report by Minister of War Chernyshev of 13 November 1841 - "the teaching of Tajudin Effendi over the course of 6 years did not change the highlanders' attitude toward us in the slightest"

  • The amanat-and-tribute system of colonel Pullo in the seized auls of Chechnya (1836-1840) — technique Manufacturing a Collaborating Elite
    Pokrovsky showed the mechanics of the split: the office of pristav was unpaid, so only "certain representatives of strong families" could take it, and they "took advantage of the opportunity to use their power for further accumulation"; "by creating in Chechnya a local administration, Chechen by nationality, tsarism hoped to create at the same time the Chechen 'aristocracy' it so needed"
