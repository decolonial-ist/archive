Campaigns

  • The Case of Pavlo Polubotok (1723)
    Peter I's establishment of the First Little Russian Collegium to seize governance from the hetman's administration: the collegium was created "as the highest appellate instance and the administrative-controlling organ of the Russian state structure in Ukraine"
  • Forced Labor on Imperial Construction: The Ladoga Canal (1704–1725)
    The chief of these was the Ladoga Canal: Peter I handed the direction of the works to Münnich, while the digging itself "was carried out chiefly by the hands of Ukrainian Cossacks," and "on the construction works, tens of thousands of people perished from back-breaking labor, hunger and the harsh climate"
  • Creation of the Little Russian Prikaz (1663)
    The Little Russian Prikaz was founded "as one of the departments of the Ambassadorial Prikaz to administer Ukrainian affairs," and through it the tsarist government "exercised control over the political-administrative activity of the hetman's administration and the tsarist voivodes in Ukraine"
  • Creation of the Little Russian Governorate-General (1801–1811)
    Administrative absorption: "the LITTLE RUSSIAN GOVERNORATE-GENERAL – an administrative-territorial and political unit of the Russian Empire, created in 1802 for the incorporation and integration of Left-Bank Ukraine into the Russian Empire"
    The outcome is recorded there as well: "During the existence of the governorate-general, the remnants of the Hetmanate's autonomy were finally liquidated"
  • Abolition of Magdeburg Rights (1831)
    The abolition of the traditional system of municipal self-government: "The final liquidation of Magdeburg law on the western Ukrainian lands took place in 1786, and in the cities of Central and Left-Bank Ukraine – in 1831 (in Kyiv – 1834)"

  • The Case of Pavlo Polubotok (1723) — technique Installation of a New System of Governance
    The introduction of the collegium as a parallel organ of power to seize judicial and fiscal functions from the hetman's administration: it acted "as the highest appellate instance and administrative-controlling organ"
    and "the Little Russian Collegium for the first time channeled Ukrainian finances into the tsar's treasury in full"
  • Creation of the Little Russian Prikaz (1663) — technique Installation of a New System of Governance
    The creation of a specialized agency to govern the autonomy: through the Little Russian Prikaz the tsarist government "exercised control over the political-administrative activity of the hetman's administration and the tsarist voivodes in Ukraine"
  • Creation of the Little Russian Governorate-General (1801–1811) — technique Administrative-Territorial Division
    The erasure of historical borders and the introduction of the imperial vertical: "The governor-general concentrated the entire fullness of administrative power: he was endowed with the right of legislative initiative, determined the degree to which all-Russian laws extended within the governorate-general, and controlled the activity of state institutions, including judicial ones"
  • Abolition of Magdeburg Rights (1831) — technique Installation of a New System of Governance
    The abolition of traditional local self-government: "The final liquidation of Magdeburg law… in the cities of Central and Left-Bank Ukraine – in 1831 (in Kyiv – 1834)"
  • Abolition of Magdeburg Rights (1831) — technique Exclusion of the Native Language from Official Use
    The displacement of the native language from the official sphere of the cities: with the liquidation of the remnants of Magdeburg self-government, the last institutional support for the local legal tradition disappeared – Ukrainian-language court proceedings had been curtailed as early as the extension of the all-Russian judicial system in the 1780s
