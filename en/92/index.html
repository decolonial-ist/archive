How the text was obtained

kabardino_russkie_otnosheniya_v_xvi_xviii_vv_dokumenty_i_materialy_tom_i_1957_kabardino_russkie_otnosheniya.txt — the text was recognised by us, at the decolonial.ist archive. The base is the edition's text layer (ABBYY FineReader) in a PDF made from a poor scan. Looking at the image of each page, a language model corrected confused look-alike letters (и/п/н, к/х/в, е/с); an edit was accepted only if it changed look-alike letters, and the archaic spelling of the documents was left alone. The text was then merged with the typeset text of the same documents on the Vostochnaya Literatura website: where our text and the typeset text align, the typeset word is primary, while our line layout and folio marks remain; headings, archival references and footnotes that did not align with the typeset text are our own. Tools and models: Qwen 3.6 (35B); vostlit.info. the documents of the volume, 361 pages, 200,594 words; 16,532 words replaced from the typeset text.

How we check. A sample check of three document pages against the scan (pp. 39, 129 and 303 of the edition): 8 errors in 1,590 words, about 5 per 1,000; headings and archival references have more. Before the merge with the typeset text, our text of p. 39 had about 85 errors per 1,000 words.

What is still wrong. A 150 dpi scan: even the eye cannot tell и/п/н apart on it. Document headings, archival references and footnotes were not checked against the typeset text; in them "№" is often read as "А?". Where the typeset text is wrong, its error passed into ours ("Мудал-князь" where the scan has "Муцал-князь"). The edition's introduction, commentary and indexes are not in the file. Report an error: contact@decolonial.ist.

First publication. This is the first time this text is published in recognised form: all the documents of the volume as one text; online the volume existed only as a scan with a poor text layer and as typeset individual documents on vostlit.info.
