  • First Russian-Chechen War (1994–1996) — technique Military Intervention
    On 24 March Russian troops broke into Achkhoy-Martan, a district center in the west of Ichkeria, but fighters of the army of Ichkeria liberated the village with a counterattack
    On 3 June the occupiers captured Vedeno, a district center to the southeast of Grozny, and on 12 June Shatoy to the south of the capital and Nozhai-Yurt in the southeast
    In three days of fighting the army of Ichkeria retook the center and about 80 percent of the city
    Russian units still stood in Shali, near Bamut and in the Naur and Shelkovskaya districts in the north of Ichkeria
  • First Russian-Chechen War (1994–1996) — technique Puppet Government
    On 7 August Zavgayev fled to the base of the occupation troops in Khankala, on the eastern outskirts of the city
    At the end of August the police officers of the puppet government moved from their buildings to the Russian military at the Severny airport to the north of the capital
