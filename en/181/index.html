Procedures

  • Russification of Education and Religion (1769–1786) — technique Conversion of Schools to the Metropole's Language
    The unification was completed by the order of February 13, 1786: "to adopt immediately for the Kyiv Academy the mode of instruction legalized for all schools in the All-Russian Empire"
  • Russification of Education and Religion (1769–1786) — technique Ban on Worship in the Native Language
    and in 1786 metropolitan Myslavsky prescribed to the clergy the unification of pronunciation and orthography along the Russian model
  • The Ems Ukaz and Total Censorship (1876–1887) — technique Censorship
    The censor's refusal to consider national manuscripts: "On a Ukrainian grammar prepared in Russian, the censor wrote the resolution: 'It would be naive to hope for permission to print the grammar of a language that must not exist'"
  • The Ems Ukaz and Total Censorship (1876–1887) — technique Denial of a Distinct Identity
    The ideological justification of the censorship refusal – the language was declared nonexistent by definition: the censor's resolution about "the grammar of a language that must not exist"
  • Ban on the Language in Books and Public Speeches (1892–1905) — technique Censorship
    "The ban on importing Ukrainian books from abroad was confirmed in the harshest form in 1894"
  • Ban on the Language in Books and Public Speeches (1892–1905) — technique Destruction of Local Knowledge Systems
    — Forbid"
  • Financial Russification of Schools (1978–1983) — technique Cultural Chauvinism
    …conference "The Russian Language — the Language of Friendship and Cooperation of the Peoples of the USSR" (Tashkent, 22–24 May 1979) adopted detailed recommendations on Russification - from organizing "the study of the Russian language in the senior groups of preschool institutions" to "the use of the Russian language in lessons in other school subjects" in the senior grades of national schools
  • Financial Russification of Schools (1978–1983) — technique Financial Discrimination Against Teachers
    …the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Ukraine and the Council of Ministers of the Ukrainian SSR, by a resolution of 10 June 1983 - signed by Shcherbytsky and Liashko, stamped "Not for the press" - raised from 1984 by 15 percent the salary rates of teachers of Russian language and literature and allowed classes of more than 25 pupils to be split into subgroups for Russian lessons
