Campaigns

  • Annexation of the Kyiv Metropolis by the Moscow Patriarchate (1685–1690)
    The subordination of the church was justified by geopolitics: "Golitsyn's main project… was the reform of the Ukrainian Orthodox church, or rather – the subordination of the Kyiv metropolitanate to the Moscow patriarch"
  • "Eternal Peace" with Poland (1686)
    "Russian historians usually assess the Eternal Peace as Golitsyn's great diplomatic success, Polish ones – as their major defeat, and contemporary Ukrainian ones – as a national tragedy of Ukraine"
  • Mazepa's Defection to Sweden and the Baturyn Massacre (1708)
    Peter ordered: "and Baturyn, as a sign to the traitors (since they resisted), burn down entirely as an example to others"
    and in total "about 15 thousand Ukrainians died in the Baturyn tragedy, including all the women and children"
  • Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
    A military operation of Muscovite troops to completely destroy the Zaporozhian (Chortomlyk) Sich as an autonomous military center in revenge for its support of Charles XII: "Having lost the proxy battle for Zaporizhzhia, Peter decided to follow the path of Baturyn — to destroy the Sich"
