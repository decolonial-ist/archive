  Text of Yeltsin Decree on Chechnya Troop Withdrawal: "On Measures to Ensure
                Further Peaceful Settlement in the Chechen Republic"
ITAR·TASS, November 25, 1996

FBIS-SOV-96-229

Daily Report 25 Nov 1996

[FBIS Transcribed Text] MOSCOW, November 25 (Itar-Tass)

For the purpose of implementing the programme of action to reach accord and reconciliation in
society, ensuring a further peaceful settlement in the Chechen Republic and creating conditions for
all citizens, who have the right to participate in the formation of bodies of state power in the
Chechen Republic, to exercise their right to vote, I hereby decree:

1. To redeploy the 101st detached brigade of Interior Ministry troops of the Russian Federation and
the 205th detached brigade of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation beyond the
administrative border of the Chechen Republic.

2. To instruct the Government of the Russian Federation:

-- to ensure the planned redeployment of the 101st detached brigade of Interior Ministry troops and
205th detached brigade of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation;

-- to take the necessary measures to prepare places for the deployment of the above brigades and
ensure social welfare and everyday services for their personnel and the dependents of officers and
warrant-officers.

-- to determine the status and terms of using the Severny airport (Groznyy).

3. To instruct the minister of defence of the Russian Federation and the minister of internal affairs of
the Russian Federation to submit, for approval, a plan of deployment of the brigades speci1ied in
clause of this Decree in the North Caucasian region.

4. To instruct the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Russian Federation to take, with the consent of
bodies of power in the Chechen Republic, measures to ensure the security of t}Je population and
vital facilities on the territory of the Chechen Republic.

5. Decree of the President of the Russian Federation No. 663 of July 3, 1996 'On the Deployment of
Military Management Bodies, Forces, Military Units, Institutions and Organisations of the Armed
Forces of the Russian Federation on the Territory of the Chechen Republic' (Code of Laws of the
Russian Federation, 1995, No.28, Article 2640) shall become null and void.

6. This Decree enters into force on the date of signature.

President of the Russian Federation

Boris Yeltsin

Moscow, the Kremlin

November 23, 1996

No.1590.
