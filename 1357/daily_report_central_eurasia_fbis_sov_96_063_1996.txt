(FBIS Translated Text} Esteemed compatriots! As |
promised, today, on 31 Mach, I will reveal to you the
program for settling the crisis in the Chechen Republic,
which over a year ago turned into an armed conflict.
Because of the extreme importance of today’s staiement
on settling the Chechen problem, | decided to invite
esteemed journalists to answer their questions after the
Statement.

The Chechen crisis is Russia's most difficult problem.
There is no simple way of ending this conflict, nor could
there be. It needed a lot of time to thoughtfully study all
the proposals and assess possible versions of our actions
and their consequences. Two commissions were set up
with the most highly qualified experts participating in
their work, which resulted in a program for settling
the crisis in the Chechen Republic. It was discussed
twice by the Security Council, and after the finishing
touches were put to it, it was adopted. Now this program
comes into action. It envisages a system of measures that
are all directed toward restoring peace, tranquillity, and
stability to the Chechen Republic. The program will be
implemented through the joint efforts of the federal and
republican bodies of power with support and help from
all the component parts of the federation and tre whole
population.

First of all, in accordance with the program, as of
midnight on 31 March 1996, military operations in
the territory of Chechnya will be stopped. Let me
stress that the actions of the troops allowed to develop
the necessary prerequisites for a radical turning point
in the situation. A stage-by-stage withdrawal of the
federal forces from the quiet regions in Chechnya to its
administrative borders is starting. The scheme for the
redeployment of the troops is ready. At the same time
the Chechen leadership will work to widen the zones
of accord, safety, and peace in the Chechen Republic.
Today they comprise almost two thirds of its territory. It
goes without saying that we will not stand for terrorist
acts. There will be adequate responses to them.

The safety of Russia's citizens must be ensured rigor-
ously. But these actions cannot and will not be a substi-
tute for the political solutions that are envisaged in the
program for a settlement to the crisis. Therefore, the
second task is to prepare for and to hold throughout the
whole territory of Chechnya free democratic elections
