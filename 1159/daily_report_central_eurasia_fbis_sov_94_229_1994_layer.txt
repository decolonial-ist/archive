Migrants
MM2811150594 Moscow ROSSIYSKAYA GAZETA in
Russian 25 Nov 94 Weekend Edition p 4
(Unattributed report incorporating statements by Galina

Federation Council Committee for International Affairs;
date, place of interviews not given: “Border Regions’

[FBIS Translated Text] The Agreement on Aid to Refugees and Enforced Resettlers, signed in Moscow 24
September 1993, has been ratified.
Following the collapse of the USSR, many of our compatriots found themselves “abroad” and in a difficult position. A flood of refugees swept over Russia from virtually
all CIS and Baltic countries. Russia was faced with another
serious problem: How to provide facilities for its compatriots?
“According to expert assessments,” Galina Karelova,
chairman of the Federation Council Committee for
Social Policy, said, commenting on the situation, “we
can expect a deluge of 2-3 million people onto the
territory of Russia from the states of the new [novyy]
abroad in the near future.”
Last year, for every 100 people who left Russia, 250
arrived, and 80 percent of these were Russians. The
agreement envisages commitments by the state to those
leaving to compensate for the property and housing left
behind, to settle mutual accounts, and to reimburse
expenditure on supporting these categories of people.

the Leningrad Naval Base, as well as a visit to Kaliningrad Oblast where the guest will meet with Baltic Fleet

this region is quite understandable. However, people in
this country do not forget other parts of Europe either.

[Interview with Dmitriy Volkogonov, military adviser to
President Yeltsin, by Enrico Franceschini in Moscow;

NATO; ‘Bombing a Mistake’”’]

one of Yeltsin’s close collaborators, General Dmitriy
Volkogonov, a Kremlin adviser on military issues, confirmed that the bombings of Bosnia “are the wrong

there is now a unanimity of positions on the conflict in
the former Yugoslavia: positions which, with each

maintained so far by the United States and the West.

from journalists. Answering questions at the end of the
talks Army Gen. Pavel Grachev expressed concern that
the embargo on shipments of arms and military hardware to the warring parties in Bosnia has effectively been

more rapidly.

implementation to mutual advantage of the accumulated
potential in the commercial, economic, scientific, technical, humanitarian and other relations.

striving to help enhance efficiency of the United Nations
and promote the widening of cooperation in the AsiaPacific region.
