Only quite recently the couniry was extracting more

There seemed to be no particular need for concern

guarantee that energy would reach consumers in the

Today the situation in the fuel and energy sphere is
radically different from before. Oil and coal extraction
in Russia has fallen dramatically, the integrated energy

that hamper the development of the CIS countries’

The Russian doctrine is characterized by the fact that

Day celebrated by Lithuania for the first time.

was restored, democratic reforms are being carried out
in the republic; the country has ensured the main rights
and freedoms of man, and the national minorities live in
peace and accord.” Brazauskas also said that Lithuania is
ore of few countries of Europe which signed agreements
on neighborliness with all countries it borders on.

ideals of the European civilization, that is why Europe
is the only space in which our people can preserve its
distrinctive features,” he said.

European Union also by the fact that this will help not
only to expand the zone of stability and well-being in
Central Europe, but will also strengthen the security of
Lithuania itself. He expects that a EU membership will
attract foreign investments into the republic and will
enable it to join the international division of labor.

But the problem remains, a problem which is becoming
very acute for Lithuania. The republic has around 800
illegal migrants in detention. What is to be done with
them?

Republic,” which guarantees asylum and certain social
rights, was adopted quite recently. However, there is
no single place of detention for the migrants; they are
scattered throughout the republic. And with the onset of
summer Lithuania will again face a mass influx of Asian
refugees, who mainly enter the republic from Russia and
Belarus.

get to the West. Lithuania is a transit center. Certain
groups of “businessmen” capitalize on people's lives and
misfortune. One professor of chemistry from Pakistan
