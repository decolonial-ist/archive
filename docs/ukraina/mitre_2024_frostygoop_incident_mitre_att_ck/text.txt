FrostyGoop Incident

 

 

FrostyGoop Incident

 

 

FrostyGoop Incident
 took place in January 2024 against a municipal district heating company in Ukraine. Following initial access via likely exploitation of external facing services, 
FrostyGoop
 was used to manipulate ENCO control systems via legitimate Modbus commands to impact the delivery of heating services to Ukrainian civilians.
