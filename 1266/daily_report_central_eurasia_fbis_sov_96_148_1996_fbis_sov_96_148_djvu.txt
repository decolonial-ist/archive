The Russian Federation Defense Council (hereinafter
the Defense Council) is the standing consultative organ
preparing the Russian Federation president's decisions
in the sphere of military organizational development and
the implementation of the highly important decisions
of the Russian Federation Security Council on strategic
issues of defense policy.

2. The Defense Council elaborates decisions on impor-
tant issues such as measures to prepare for the protection
of the Russian Federation from armed attack or the di-
rect protection of the Russian Federation from armed

attack, the elaboration of an overall concept of military
organizational development, the coordination and mon-
itoring of the activity of the Russian Federation Armed
Forces and other troops and military formations, and the
definition of their mission, composition, and size in ac-
cordance with the Russian Federation Constitution, the
federal law "On Defense,” other Russian Federation acts
of legislation and international treaties, and this statute.

3. The Statute on the Defense Council is ratified by the
Russian Federation president following representations
from the secretary of the Russian Federation Defense
Council (hereinafter the Defense Council secretary).

Il. Composition of the Defense Counci!

4. The Defense Council comprises the chairman o. the
Russian Federation Defense Council (hereinafter the
Defense Council chairman), the deputy chairman of
the Russian Federation Defense Council (hereinafter the
Defense Council deputy chairman), the Defense Council
secretary, and members of the Defense Council.

5. The Defense Council chairman is the Russian Fed-
eration president, the Defense Council deputy chairman
is the chairman of the Russian Federation Government.

The Defense Council secretary is appointed by the
Russian Federation president.

The composition of the Defense Council is ratified by
the Russian Federation president.

The leaders of state organs, organizations, and other
persons directly involved with the issues at hand may
be invited to Defense Council sessions.

V. Procedure of the Defense Council’s Work

11. Defense Council sessions are held at least once
a month. i council sessions may be held,
if necessary, at the decision of the Defense Council
chairman.

Defense Council members have equal rights in decision-
making.

Defense Council members are not allowed to delegate
their powers to other officials. A Defense Council
member can be temporarily replaced only with the
consent of the Defense Council chairman or his deputy.

If the Defense Council chairman is temporarily absent,
his duties are performed by the Defense Council deputy
chairman. On the instructions of the Defense Council
chairman Defense Council sessions are chaired by the
Defense Council deputy chairman.

Defense Council sessions are minuted and the minutes
are signed by the Defense Council chairman and the
Defense Council secretary.

Defense Council decisions on the most important issues
are formulated in Russian Federation presidential edicts
and directives.

VL Defense Council Apparatus

12. Defense Council activity is ensured by the Defense
Council Apparatus.
The Defense Council Apparatus is part of the Russian

Federation Presidential Staff and has the status of a main
administration of the Russian Federation president.

13. The permanent staff of the Defense Council Ap-
paratus is ratified by the leader of the Russian Feder-
ation Presidential Staff following representations from
the Defense Council secretary.

14. The Defense Council involves federal executive or-
gans and the Russian Federation Armed Forces General
Staff in carrying out certain tasks.

Estonia: Government Proposes President Be
Elected 26 Aug

LD3007164096 Moscow INTERFAX in English
1432 GMT 30 Jul 96

(FBIS Transcribed Text] TALLINN, July 30 (Interfax)
— The Estonian government has proposed Tuesday that
parliament hold an extraordinary session on August 26
to elect the president of the country.

The elections will be held in connection with the end of
the office term of incumbent Estonian President Lennart
Meri, Estonian Prime Minister Tiit Vahi told a news
conference in Tallinn.

Under the electoral law, Estonian presidents are elected
by parliament. For the president to be elected, 68 of the
101 deputies must vote for his candidacy. If none of the
contenders gets the required number of votes, the issue
is resolved by an electoral college including parliament
members and 250 local representatives.

The incumbent president and Deputy Parliament Chair-
man Arnold Ruutel are considered the most likely can-
didates for the post now.

The government also proposed that the parliament hold
another extraordinary session on August 15, said Vahi.

The session would discuss ratification of a treaty on
the sea border between Estonia and Latvia and a treaty
between Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania on free trade in
agricultural goods.
