(FBIS Transcribed Text] Kiev, March 4 — The draft of
a new Ukrainian constitution has been completed and
will be reviewed by a Constitutional Committee con-
sisting of presidential and parliamentary representatives
on Monday.

The draft, as it stands now, is largely a victory for
President Leonid Kuchma’s point of view, accepting as
it does his proposals for a bicameral legislature and a
strong presidency.

The new constitution says that Ukraine is a unitary
country, with Ukrainian language as both its state and
official language. The national flag will be the familiar
blue and yellow banner, while the trident will continue
as the state emblem. The national anthem is “Ukraine
Has Not Died Yet.”

According to Constitstional Committee members, the
draft envisions a two-house legislature to be called the
Nationa! Legislative Assembly. In comparison with ear-
lier drafts, the current constitutional proposal increases
the size of the lower house from 220 to 370 members.

The new wording also increases presidential power by
eliminating the right of legislative initiative from the
Cabinet of Ministers and the Crimean Autonomous
government. If the new constitution is approved under
its current formulation, only the President, Senators
and Deputies will have the right to submit laws to the
legislature.

Ukrainian politicians have so far been mixed in their
reaction to the proposals.

Vitaliy Juravskiy, head of the Christian Democratic
Party, criticized the new constitution for putting “the
President at the head of executive power” while “he
will not bear any responsibility.”

On the other hand, Oleksandr Rozumkov, head of the
expert council of the center for political and economic
investigation and a former presidential administration
worker, said that the most recent constitutional draft
“ensures the necessary balance between all branches of
power.”
