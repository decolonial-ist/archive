2016 Ukraine Electric Power Attack

 

 

2016 Ukraine Electric Power Attack
 was a 
Sandworm Team
 campaign during which they used 
Industroyer
 malware to target and disrupt distribution substations within the Ukrainian power grid. This campaign was the second major public attack conducted against Ukraine by 
Sandworm Team
.
