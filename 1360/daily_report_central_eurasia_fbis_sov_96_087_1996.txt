Study Shows Little Room for Growth in Yeltsin's Pop-
ularity A report issued by the Vox Populi and 14 public
opinion organizations in March indicates that Yeltsin
faces an uphill battle in improving his popularity in
many regions. The report, published in NEZAVISI-
MAYA GAZETA (20 April), identified 17 distinct re-
gions in the country and contended that only three ot
these regions support “democracy,” while 11 support
communism,” and four favor “national-patriotism.” Ac-
cording to the study, Yeltsin's popularity has room to
grow in the European North, St. Petersburg, Moscow,
the Urals, and North- and Mid-Western Siberia. Con-
versely, his ratings have a high potential for going down

in the Black Earth region and surrounding provinces oi

central Russia, the Southern Volga region, the South, the
Northern Caucasus, Southwestern Siberia, and the Far
East. Zyuganov has room for growth in the Northwest,
the Black Earth region and surrounding provinces, Cen-
tral and Southern Volga region, the South, the North-
ern Caucasus, Southwestern Siberia, and Soutiieastern
Siberia, while his ratings could go down in the Euro-
pean North, St. Petersburg, Moscow, the Urals, North-
western and Midwestern Siberia, and Northeastern and
Mideastern Siberia
